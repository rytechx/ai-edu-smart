QUESTIONNAIRE
The system uses one neutral 15-item Student Engagement Questionnaire for:
- Experimental Pre
- Experimental Post
- Control Pre
- Control Post

DIMENSIONS
Items 1-5: Behavioral Engagement
Items 6-10: Cognitive Engagement
Items 11-15: Emotional Engagement

LIKERT SCALE
4 = Strongly Agree
3 = Agree
2 = Disagree
1 = Strongly Disagree

AUTOMATIC SCORING
Behavioral Mean = average of Items 1-5
Cognitive Mean = average of Items 6-10
Emotional Mean = average of Items 11-15
Overall Mean = average of Items 1-15

INTERPRETATION
3.25-4.00 = Strongly Agree
2.50-3.24 = Agree
1.75-2.49 = Disagree
1.00-1.74 = Strongly Disagree

RECOMMENDED RESEARCH SEQUENCE
BEFORE INTERVENTION
1. Assign research groups.
2. Open Achievement Pretest.
3. Open Pre-Engagement questionnaire.
4. Confirm submissions.
5. Close both.

INTERVENTION
Experimental:
- AI Edu Smart learning intervention

Control:
- Conventional instruction

AFTER INTERVENTION
1. Open Achievement Posttest.
2. Open Post-Engagement questionnaire.
3. Confirm submissions.
4. Close both.
5. Export achievement CSV.
6. Export engagement CSV.

CSV EXPORT
Admin > Engagement Research > Export CSV

The CSV contains:
- Student Code
- Student Name
- Section
- Research Group
- Pre Items 1-15
- Pre Behavioral/Cognitive/Emotional/Overall means
- Post Items 1-15
- Post Behavioral/Cognitive/Emotional/Overall means
- Dimension changes
- Overall change

NOTE
System descriptive comparisons are not the final inferential statistical analysis.
Use the exported data for the study's approved statistical treatment.
